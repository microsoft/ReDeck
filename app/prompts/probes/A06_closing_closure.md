# A06: Weak Closing — closing_closure

## Focus
Does the ending complete the story opened at the start of the deck?

## Pass if
1. The final slide closes the loop on the opening objective
2. The audience is left with a conclusion, implication, or recommendation
3. The ending does more than stop after the last piece of evidence
4. Any unresolved uncertainty is framed intentionally

## Fail if
1. The deck ends abruptly without synthesis
2. The closing slide repeats prior bullets without adding implications
3. The ending does not connect back to the stated purpose
4. The deck stops on a detail slide and feels unfinished

## Do not flag
- A brief ending if it provides real closure

## Severity
- critical when there is no closing synthesis and the deck just stops
- major when a closing exists but fails to connect to the opening
- minor when the closing is adequate but could be stronger

## Boundary — use another probe instead
- A06 = narrative closure quality (the ending doesn't feel complete)
- C05 = factual completeness (a specific required conclusion is absent from the source)
- Do not duplicate a C05 finding as A06

## Evidence requirements
- Compare the opening objective with the closing slide language
- Note presence or absence of summary, recommendation, or implication
