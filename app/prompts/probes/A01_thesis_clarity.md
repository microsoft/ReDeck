# A01: Weak Thesis — thesis_clarity

## Focus
Does the deck communicate one clear overall objective, question, or message?

## Pass if
1. An identifiable central objective is present
2. The objective remains stable across the deck
3. Major slides support the central objective
4. The audience can summarize the deck's point in 1–2 sentences

## Fail if
1. Multiple competing purposes exist without a clear hierarchy
2. The main point is too vague to act on or remember
3. Early slides and late slides appear to serve different purposes
4. The deck reads as source-dumping without a unifying thread

## Do not flag
- A nuanced thesis with sub-questions that roll up to one overarching purpose
- An implicit but unmistakable objective

## Severity
- critical when no discernible purpose can be extracted
- major when purpose exists but competes with other threads or is too vague
- minor when purpose is present but could be sharpened

## Boundary — use another probe instead
- If the issue is that the opening fails to frame the topic → A02 (missing_context)
- If the issue is slide-to-slide progression rather than overall purpose → A03 (poor_flow)

## Evidence requirements
- Cite the title slide, repeated section headers, and closing slide language
- Show whether these elements converge on a single purpose or diverge
