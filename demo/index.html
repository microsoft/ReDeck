Method

Multi-Granular Feedback with Separated Responsibilities

Different feedback sources are assigned to the levels where they are most reliable and useful.

Step
Step-Level Render FeedbackLocal spatial repair

After each atomic edit, renderer-derived observations report immediate spatial facts before the next action. Observation, not verdict.

Turn
Turn-Level Adaptive Deck CriticGlobal semantic and design guidance

Maintains a persistent issue list for narrative flow, content completeness, factual correctness, source fidelity, and visual design.

Submit
Submission-Level Validation GateHard layout validation

Ensures the total hard-violation count does not increase relative to the session baseline before a turn is committed.
