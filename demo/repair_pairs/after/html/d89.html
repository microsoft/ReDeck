Executive Takeaways

  • Solar remains the lowest-cost growth engine, but interconnection queues are extending commissioning by 2–3 quarters in key U.S. markets.
  • Wind asset availability recovered after Q1 maintenance, lifting blended capacity factor and narrowing the gap to annual generation targets.
  • Battery economics strengthened as ancillary service prices stayed elevated during evening peak periods.
