Key findings
  • Onshore wind remains the lowest-cost zero-carbon option in median tenders, but revenue quality weakens in saturated midday markets.
  • Firmed solar closes much of the capture-price gap and outperforms standalone PV on dispatchability, especially in summer peak windows.
  • Offshore wind and geothermal deliver higher system value where winter reliability and evening demand are heavily weighted.
Method note
Sample includes 61 projects announced between Q4 2023 and Q3 2024. LCOE values normalized to a 7.2% WACC, 25-year asset life, and regional interconnection charges. Capture price estimates use hourly nodal pricing with curtailment assumptions from operator filings.
Source: IEA procurement trackers, developer filings, Aurora market simulations
