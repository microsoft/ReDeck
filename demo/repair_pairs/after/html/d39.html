What matters now
  • Hybridization is no longer optional: storage consistently raises capture price and lowers imbalance risk in volatile evening ramps.
  • Interconnection queue timing has become the dominant schedule risk, especially for offshore wind repowering and large PV clusters.
  • Procurement teams should prioritize flexible offtake structures over headline PPA price minimization when congestion risk is elevated.
Board implication: rebalance 2025 pipeline toward solar-plus-storage and defer standalone offshore commitments until transmission upgrade visibility improves materially
