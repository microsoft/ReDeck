Interpretation
Utility solar remains the cheapest incremental supply option, but portfolio economics improve materially when paired with storage or complemented by geothermal in systems with steep evening ramps and winter demand peaks.
Procurement implication
Buyers targeting both decarbonization and reliability are shifting from single-technology tenders toward blended renewable stacks, with storage duration and interconnection certainty now outweighing pure nameplate scale.
