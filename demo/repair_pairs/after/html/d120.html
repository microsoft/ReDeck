Interpretation

The bar chart shows a widening spread between mature renewables and capital-intensive baseload pathways. Utility solar remains the lowest-cost option in high-irradiance regions, while onshore wind preserves a favorable position where transmission access is already built. Advanced nuclear and offshore wind both improve system resilience, but their economics remain sensitive to financing rates and construction risk.

What moved this quarter

Turbine pricing stabilized, battery-coupled solar interconnection queues shortened modestly, and steel-heavy projects saw less relief than expected. The result is a flatter cost curve for wind and a clearer lead for solar in merchant procurement discussions.
